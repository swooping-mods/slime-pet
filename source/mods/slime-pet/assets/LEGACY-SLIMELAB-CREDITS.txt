Modifications for Moon Sweepers Slime Pet: normalized axes/metric scale and ground
origin; subdivision and skin baked to sampled mesh poses; compact quantization;
Blender inverted outline removed; material adaptation; original transparent face
card identified for runtime replacement; animated face attachment anchors added.
Source author does not endorse this mod. This attribution must accompany copies
of the derived mesh/animation data and retained free Green body textures.

Original texture names: GreenSlimeColor.png, GreenSlimeEmision.png.
The Basic .blend does not include its referenced body/eye texture files. The
supplementary Green textures are optional; the embedded C defaults use explicit
colors. The original face-card texture was unavailable and is not reconstructed.
